## What changes per market

## Process

Translations live in the `de` and `fr` fields. Every machine translation gets `translationStatus: needs_review`; a translation with `reviewed` is never overwritten.

---

With this skill, "Waxed Tote, Olive: a tough commuter tote in waxed canvas" becomes **"Waxed Tote, Olive: eine robuste Pendlertasche aus gewachstem Canvas"**, product name kept, glossary term used, marked needs review.
