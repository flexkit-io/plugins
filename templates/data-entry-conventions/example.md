# Data entry conventions: Northline Goods

## Names

Products are "Type, Colour" in Title Case: "Linen Shirt, Sand". Size is a variant, never part of the name. No marketing words ("New", "Bestseller").

## SKUs

`TYPE-COLOUR-SIZE`, uppercase three-letter codes: `LS-SND-M`. Unique across all variants; archived SKUs are never reused.

## Units and formats

- Weight in grams, whole numbers. Dimensions in cm, width × height × depth.
- Prices in GBP including VAT, two decimals.
- Phone numbers in E.164 (+44…), countries as ISO codes (GB, DE).

## Required fields

A product needs name, category, price, weight, a SKU on every variant and at least one image before it is published.

## Variants

Size and colour are variants; a different material is a new product. Sizes run XS, S, M, L, XL.

## Never

No "TBD" or placeholder text. No second customer record for an email that already has one.

---

With this skill, a new product the agent creates from a supplier sheet reads **"Waxed Tote, Olive"** with variant SKU **WXT-OLV-OS**, weight **640** (g), not "NEW Waxed Canvas Tote Bag - Olive Green (one size)".
