# Publishing checklist: Northline Goods

## How "live" is recorded

Products: `status` `active` is live; `draft` and `archived` are not. Journal articles: `publishedAt` set and in the past.

## Products

- Name follows the data entry conventions
- Price set; margin passes the pricing policy
- At least 3 images, each with alt text
- Description of at least 80 words, 3–5 bullets
- SEO title ≤60 characters, meta description ≤155
- Weight in grams; every variant has a SKU

## Journal articles

Hero image with alt text, meta description, an author, no "TBD", no links to archived products.

## Translations

Live products have German and French text. A translation marked `needs_review` blocks only that locale.

## Sign-off

New products: Priya (buying). Campaign pages: Jo (marketing).

## Stale content

Not updated for 6 months, mentions a past year, or links to an archived product.
