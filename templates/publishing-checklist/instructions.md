Use this whenever you check whether a record is ready to go live, or review what is already live. A record is ready only when every item for its type passes. Report each failing item with the field and what is missing; do not publish or unpublish anything yourself unless you are asked to.

[[The field and values that mean live, draft and archived, per entity.]]

> Example: Products: `status` is `active` (live), `draft` or `archived`. Articles: `publishedAt` is set and in the past.

## Products

- [[Each item that must be true, as a check against real fields]]

> Example:
> - Name follows the data entry conventions
> - Price set, and the margin passes the pricing policy
> - At least 3 images, the first on a white background, each with alt text
> - Description of at least 80 words, and 3–5 bullet points
> - SEO title (≤60 characters) and meta description (≤155)
> - Weight set, so shipping can be calculated
> - Every variant has a SKU and stock tracking on
