# Setting up the publishing checklist

## Read first

- For each entity that is published (products, pages, articles, collections): the field that marks it live, and its values.
- Compare 10 live records with 10 drafts of the same entity: which fields are always filled on live records (these are the de facto requirements), image counts, description lengths, SEO fields, translations.
- Whether the project has the data entry conventions, pricing and discount policy, product copy guidelines or translation skills. Refer to them from the checklist ("Name follows the data entry conventions") instead of repeating their rules.
- Project knowledge documents about launches, QA or publishing.

Report the de facto requirements you found ("Every live product has at least 3 images and a weight; 4 of 10 drafts have no weight").

## Interview

Ask in one or two rounds, offering what you found as defaults:
1. For each type of content, confirm the checks, and add any the data cannot show (photo style, legal text).
2. What does translated mean here: all locales, or the main ones?
3. Who signs off each type before it goes live?
4. When is live content stale?
