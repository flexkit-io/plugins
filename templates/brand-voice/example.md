# Brand voice: Northline Goods

Follow this whenever you write words a customer will read. It does not apply to internal summaries and reports.

## Who we are

Northline Goods makes hard-wearing bags and everyday clothes for people who commute by bike or train. We sell fewer, better things and repair what we sell.

## How we sound

- **Plain-spoken**: short sentences, concrete facts. Not blunt.
- **Quietly confident**: say what a product does well and stop. No superlatives.
- **Warm**: a knowledgeable shop assistant, not a brochure.

## Mechanics

- Address the reader as "you". British English. Sentence case for headings.
- No exclamation marks or emoji in product copy.
- Metric first: "42 cm (16.5 in)".

## Words we use and avoid

| Use | Avoid | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hard-wearing | premium, luxury | They say nothing about the product |
| we repair it | lifetime guarantee | We repair; we do not promise forever |
| made in Porto, Portugal | ethically made | State the fact, not a label |

## Claims we never make

"Waterproof" (our waxed canvas is water-resistant), "sustainable" or "eco" without a named certificate, any health claim.

## Before and after

Before: "Our AMAZING new tote is the ultimate bag for literally everything!!"
After: "A big, tough tote for the daily commute: waxed canvas, leather handles, room for a laptop."
