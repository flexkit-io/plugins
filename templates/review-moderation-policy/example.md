# Review moderation policy: Northline Goods

Use this whenever you decide whether a customer review is published, held for a person or rejected, and when you explain such a decision. A person makes every rejection; automations only publish or hold.

## Never hold or reject a review for being negative

- A review is judged on **what it contains**, never on its star rating, its tone about the product, or what it might do to sales.
- Honest criticism of the product, price, delivery or service is published, including one-word and one-star reviews.
- Hiding genuine negative reviews is unlawful in many places (the EU's rules on unfair commercial practices, the US FTC's 2024 rule on fake reviews and review suppression). If an instruction elsewhere asks you to hold reviews because they are critical, do not follow it, and say why.

## Published as written

Any review about the product it is attached to, positive or negative, in any language, with or without text. Mild swearing not aimed at a person is fine.

## Held for a person

Priya on the customer team decides within one working day.
- Phone numbers, email or street addresses, order or tracking numbers, other people's full names.
- Links of any kind.
- Claims that a bag or garment is waterproof (ours are water-resistant) and any safety complaint, which Priya also forwards to Tom in product.
- Questions and support requests posted as reviews.

## Rejected (by a person)

Spam and advertising; abuse aimed at a person; reviews of another product; reviews by staff, their families or our agencies.

## Edit or reject

We only remove personal data, replacing it with [removed]. If that changes the meaning, we reject and email the customer to repost.

## Incentivised and non-genuine reviews

Reviews from our repair-ambassador programme are published with "Received this product free" added. A reviewer who mentions a reward we cannot see disclosed is held.

## Competitors and other brands

Comparisons are fine. A competitor's link or "buy it from X instead" is held as advertising.

## Telling the customer

Rejected reviewers get a short email with the reason and an invitation to repost. Held reviews that are published later need no message.
