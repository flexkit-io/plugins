# Review moderation policy: [[store name]]

Use this whenever you decide whether a customer review is published, held for a person or rejected, and when you explain such a decision. A person makes every rejection; automations only publish or hold.

## Never hold or reject a review for being negative

This section is fixed: keep it as it is when you fill in the rest.

- A review is judged on **what it contains**, never on its star rating, its tone about the product, or what it might do to sales.
- Honest criticism of the product, price, delivery or service is published, including one-word and one-star reviews.
- Hiding genuine negative reviews is unlawful in many places (the EU's rules on unfair commercial practices, the US FTC's 2024 rule on fake reviews and review suppression). If an instruction elsewhere asks you to hold reviews because they are critical, do not follow it, and say why.

## Published as written

[[What may go live without anyone checking, e.g. any review about the product it is attached to, in any language, with or without text.]]

> Example: Any review about the product, positive or negative, in any language. Mild swearing that is not aimed at a person ("bloody brilliant", "crap zip") is fine.

## Held for a person

[[What waits for someone to decide, and who that is.]]

> Example (reviewed by the customer team within one working day):
> - Personal data: phone numbers, email or street addresses, order or tracking numbers, other people's full names.
> - Links of any kind.
> - Health, medical or safety claims about our products, good or bad (a safety complaint is also passed to [[the person who handles product safety]]).
> - Support requests and questions posted as reviews.
> - Anything you are unsure about.

## Rejected (by a person)

[[What is never published.]]

> Example: Spam and advertising; abuse, threats or slurs; reviews of a different product or shop; reviews by our staff or their families; reviews we paid for without disclosure.

## Edit or reject

[[When a person may redact a review instead of rejecting it, and how an edit is shown.]]

> Example: We only ever remove personal data, replacing it with [removed], and never change anything else a customer wrote. If removing it would change the meaning, we reject the review and email the customer to repost it.

## Incentivised and non-genuine reviews

[[How reviews written for a reward are treated, e.g. free products, discounts, competitions.]]

> Example: Reviews from our product-testing programme are published with "Received this product free" added by us. A review that says it was rewarded but does not say so publicly is held. Reviews from staff, family or agencies are rejected.

## Competitors and other brands

[[How mentions of other brands are handled.]]

> Example: Comparisons ("lighter than my old Fjällräven") are fine. Recommending where to buy instead, or a competitor's link, is held as advertising.
