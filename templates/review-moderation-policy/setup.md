# Setting up the review moderation policy skill

## Read first

Before asking anything, read how the store moderates today:
- 30 recent reviews with their rating, text and status, newest first.
- Every review that is held, pending, hidden or rejected (up to 50), with any reason saved on it. These show the store's real rules better than any answer: what kind of content gets stopped, and whether low ratings are being held more often than high ones.
- Any knowledge documents about reviews, community guidelines or terms of use (search the knowledge base for "review", "moderation", "guidelines").

Note what you find: the status values in use, the share of reviews that are held or rejected, the common reasons, and whether held or rejected reviews are mostly low-rated without another reason. If they are, tell the user plainly that the current practice looks like hiding negative reviews, that this is unlawful in the EU and the US, and that the policy will not continue it.

## Interview

Ask in one or two rounds, offering what you inferred as the default answer:
1. Who decides on held reviews, and how fast?
2. What should never be published (spam, abuse, other products, staff reviews)? Confirm the examples you found.
3. Personal data and links: hold, redact, or reject?
4. Do you give products away for reviews, or run competitions? How should those reviews be labelled?
5. How do you treat mentions of competitors?
6. Is the customer told when a review is held or rejected?
