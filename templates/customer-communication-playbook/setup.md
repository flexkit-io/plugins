# Setting up the customer communication playbook

## Read first

- Policy pages or records: returns, refunds, delivery, warranty (search the project's content and knowledge base for "returns", "refund", "delivery", "warranty"). The skill must agree with what customers can already read; quote the real windows and amounts.
- Up to 10 replies the team has written, if the project stores them (review replies, support notes). Note how they open and close, what they offer, and who signs.
- Review and order schemas, if present: the fields for rating, review text, reply, order status and tracking. The automations that use this skill read them.

Summarise the policy you found back to the user and point out any gap ("Your returns page says 30 days, but doesn't say who pays return shipping").

## Interview

Ask in one or two rounds:
1. Confirm the refund and return policy you found, and what you may offer just outside it.
2. Late deliveries: after how long do you reship or refund, and what do you offer for the wait?
3. Faulty items: replace, repair or refund first?
4. How do you answer negative reviews in public?
5. Which situations must always go to a person, and who is that?
6. Anything a reply must never say or promise?
