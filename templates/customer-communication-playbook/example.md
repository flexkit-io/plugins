# Customer communication playbook: Northline Goods

## Always

First name, one specific apology, what happens next and by when, signed by a real person. Reply within one working day.

## Refunds and returns

30 days, unworn, free returns in the UK. Refunds to the original payment within 5 working days of arrival. Up to 14 days late: offer store credit. Later than that: hand over.

## Late or lost deliveries

Check tracking first. After 7 working days with no movement, reship at our cost and send a £5 code for the wait.

## Faulty or wrong items

Ask for a photo. Repair first (we repair what we sell), replacement second, refund if neither works. We always pay return shipping.

## Complaints and negative reviews

Public reply in two or three sentences: thank them, name the problem, move to email (hello@northline.example). No order details in public.

## Escalate to a person

Legal threats, chargebacks, safety issues, press, abuse, or a third message about the same problem: hand to Sam (support lead).

## Never

Promise a delivery date the carrier hasn't given, blame the carrier, offer more than above, or ask for a better review.
