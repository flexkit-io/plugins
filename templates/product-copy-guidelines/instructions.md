# Product copy guidelines: [[store name]]

Use this when you write or edit product copy: descriptions, bullet points, SEO titles and meta descriptions, and category or collection text. For tone and wording, follow the brand voice skill; this skill covers structure, facts and rules.

## Where copy goes

[[The fields that hold product copy, by their real names, and what each is for.]]

> Example: `description` (rich text, product page body), `highlights` (list of short bullets, shown beside the price), `seo.title` and `seo.description` (search results only).

## Description structure

[[The order of a description and the target length.]]

> Example:
> 1. One sentence: what it is and who it is for.
> 2. Two or three benefits, each tied to a fact (material, construction, fit).
> 3. Practical details: size, fit notes, care, what is in the box.
> Target 100–150 words. No headings inside the description.

## Facts every product must mention

[[Per category, the facts customers need before buying. Name the schema fields they come from.]]

> Example:
> - **Bags**: dimensions (`dimensions`), capacity in litres, laptop size it fits, material (`material`).
> - **Clothing**: fabric and weight, fit (slim/regular/relaxed), model's height and size worn, care.
> - **Homeware**: dimensions, material, dishwasher/microwave safe.

## Bullet points

[[Number of bullets, length, and format.]]

> Example: 4–6 bullets, each under 15 words, starting with the fact: "Waxed cotton canvas: sheds light rain".

## SEO

- **Title**: [[pattern and length, e.g. "<Product name> in <colour> | <Brand>", 50–60 characters]]
- **Meta description**: [[length and content, e.g. 140–155 characters, what it is + main benefit + "Free returns."]]
- **Keywords**: [[how to choose them, e.g. use the product type customers search for ("tote bag", not "carryall"); never repeat a keyword more than twice]]

## Never

[[What product copy must never contain.]]

> Example: prices or discounts (they change), delivery promises, competitor names, claims not backed by a product field (certifications, origin, "waterproof"), invented reviews or ratings, ALL CAPS.

## When facts are missing

[[What to do when a product lacks a fact the copy needs.]]

> Example: leave it out and list the missing facts in the run summary; never guess dimensions or materials.
