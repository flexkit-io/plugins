# Setting up product copy guidelines

## Read first

- The product schema: find the fields that hold copy (description, bullets/highlights, SEO title and meta, category text) and the fact fields (material, dimensions, care, fit). You will write their real names into the skill.
- The 10 most recently created products that have a description, across different categories if the catalogue has them. Note the usual length, structure, which facts appear, how bullets look, and how SEO titles are formed.
- Project knowledge documents about product content, SEO or style guides (search the knowledge base for "product copy", "SEO", "style guide").

Summarise what you found back to the user ("Your descriptions average 110 words, open with the product type, and mention material in 8 of 10…") and use it as the default answers.

## Interview

Ask in one or two rounds:
1. Which structure and length do you want descriptions to follow? (Offer the pattern you found.)
2. Per main category, which facts must every product mention?
3. Bullets: how many, how long?
4. SEO title and meta description rules, including the brand suffix.
5. Anything product copy must never say (legal claims, prices, competitors).
6. Which existing product page is your best example?
