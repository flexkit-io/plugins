# Product copy guidelines: Northline Goods

Use this when you write or edit product copy. For tone, follow the brand voice skill.

## Where copy goes

`description` (product page body), `highlights` (bullets beside the price), `seoTitle`, `seoDescription`.

## Description structure

1. What it is and who it is for, in one sentence.
2. Two or three benefits, each tied to a fact.
3. Size, care, what is included.

100–150 words, no headings.

## Facts every product must mention

- **Bags**: `dimensions`, capacity in litres, laptop size, `material`.
- **Clothing**: fabric and weight, fit, model's height and size worn, `care`.

## Bullet points

5 bullets, under 15 words, fact first: "Full-grain leather handles: stitched through for heavy loads".

## SEO

- Title: "<Product name> in <Colour> | Northline Goods", 50–60 characters.
- Meta description: 140–155 characters, ending with "Free returns."

## Never

Prices, delivery promises, "waterproof", "eco" or "sustainable" without a named certificate, competitor names.

## When facts are missing

Leave them out and list them in the run summary.
