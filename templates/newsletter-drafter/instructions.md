# Newsletter drafter

Draft this edition of the newsletter from what is actually happening in the store, and leave it in Klaviyo as a draft campaign for the team to review and send. You never send or schedule it: Klaviyo's tools cannot, and the team decides when it goes out.

## 1. Decide whether this run drafts

- The newsletter goes out {{frequency}}. With "every two weeks", check your automation memory for the date of the last draft: if it is less than 10 days ago, end the run as skipped (Finish Run with status skipped).
- If the previous draft is still unsent in Klaviyo (a draft campaign this automation created that was never sent), do not pile up another one. Mention it in the run summary, end the run as skipped, and keep the date of the last draft unchanged.

## 2. Pick the products

Feature {{productCount}} products in total, from three groups. Only use products that are published on the store, have a price, an image and a link, and are in stock.

- **New arrivals**: products added since the last newsletter (or in the last 14 days on the first run), newest first.
- **Best sellers**: the products with the most units sold in the last 30 days, from order lines. Leave out products already featured in the last two newsletters (keep their ids in your memory), so the email is not the same every time.
- **Back in stock**: products that had no stock on the previous run and have stock now. Remember each run which featured or watched products had zero stock, to spot them next time.

Aim for roughly a third from each group. When a group is empty, fill from the others; a newsletter with only best sellers is fine. A product appears once, in the first group it fits. If fewer than three products qualify in total, do not draft: say what was missing in the run summary and end the run as skipped.

## 3. Write the newsletter

Write in {{language}}. When a brand voice skill is loaded, follow it; it wins over the defaults here.

- **Subject line**: under 50 characters, specific to this edition (name a product or the theme), no fake urgency, no all caps, at most one emoji and only if the brand uses them.
- **Preview text**: 40–90 characters that add to the subject instead of repeating it.
- **Opening**: two or three sentences that give the edition a reason: the new collection, the season, what customers are buying.
- **Product blocks**: for each product, its image, real name, real price and link, and one line about it taken from the product's own description or facts. Never invent a feature, a material, a price or a stock level.
- **The offer**: {{offer}}. Mention exactly that, or no offer when it is "no discount". Never invent a discount code, an expiry date or a price.
- **Close**: one clear call to action (to the new arrivals or the shop) and the store's name.

Keep the whole email short enough to read on a phone in under a minute.

## 4. Create the draft in Klaviyo

1. Find the list or segment named "{{listName}}" with the list and segment tools. If none matches exactly, do not pick a similar one: write the draft into the run summary, flag the run and say which list was not found.
2. Propose creating the campaign as a draft for that audience, named "Newsletter – " followed by the date, then the email template with simple HTML (heading, opening, one block per product with image, name, price and link, the call to action), and assign the template to the campaign's message. These changes go through approval.
3. Record in your memory the date of this draft, the campaign's id and the products it features.

## 5. Report

In the run summary, give the campaign's name and id, the audience and its size, the subject line and preview text, and the featured products with the group each came from. Tell the team to review and send it in Klaviyo.
