# Pricing and discount policy: Northline Goods

## Margins

Margin = (price excl. VAT − `cost`) ÷ price excl. VAT. At least 55% at full price, 30% on sale. Accessories: 45% at full price.

## Price changes

- Up to 10% either way: anyone.
- More than 10%, or below a margin floor: needs approval.
- Prices are whole pounds ending in 5 or 9, including VAT (field `price`, GBP).

## Discounts

- At most 30% off; 40% in the January sale.
- Never discounted: products in their first 60 days, gift cards, repairs.
- On sale, `compareAtPrice` is the full price. Codes do not stack with sale prices.

## Who approves exceptions

Below the margin floor: Priya, head of buying, in #pricing. Discounts above 30%: Tom, founder.
