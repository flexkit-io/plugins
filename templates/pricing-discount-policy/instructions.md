# Pricing and discount policy: [[store name]]

Use this whenever you check, set, change or discount a price. When a price breaks a rule here, do not change it yourself: say which rule it breaks and who can approve an exception.

## Margins

- **Margin** means [[the formula, e.g. (price excluding VAT − cost) ÷ price excluding VAT]].
- **Floor**: [[the lowest margin allowed, overall or per category]]

> Example: At least 55% margin at full price and 30% on sale. Accessories may go to 45% at full price.

## Price changes

- **Normal change**: [[the size of change anyone may make, e.g. up to 10% either way]]
- **Needs approval**: [[what needs sign-off, e.g. more than 10%, or any price below the margin floor]]
- **Rounding**: [[price endings, e.g. whole pounds ending in 5 or 9: £45, £49, never £47.32]]
- **Price fields**: [[the field names for price, cost and compare-at price, and whether prices include tax]]

## Discounts

- **Largest discount**: [[e.g. 30% off, 40% in the end-of-season sale]]
- **Never discounted**: [[e.g. new arrivals in their first 60 days, gift cards, repairs]]
- **Sale prices**: [[how a sale is shown, e.g. compare-at price set to the full price; never raise the compare-at price to make a discount look bigger]]
- **Stacking**: [[whether codes stack with sale prices]]
